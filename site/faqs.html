Frequently Asked Questions about “Sobha Chikkagubbi” project.

About Sobha Chikkagubbi

What is Sobha Chikkagubbi?

Sobha Chikkagubbi is an upcoming mixed-use township by Sobha near Hennur Road in North-East Bengaluru: 4,463 homes in 13 towers of up to 42 floors on about 50.45 acres, with nine home types from serviced 1 BHK apartments to 4 BHK residences. It is in pre-launch, so register now for priority access at launch.

What makes Sobha Chikkagubbi a township rather than an apartment complex?

Its size and its mix of uses. On about 50.45 acres, the 4,463 apartments share the land with offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants in a commercial precinct, plus a clubhouse, an amenity block, parks and civic amenity land, and space is kept for education, medical care, banking and post. Many everyday errands are planned to happen without leaving the township, which sets it apart from a cluster of towers with only a clubhouse.

How is the Sobha Chikkagubbi site laid out?

The site is long and reads from west to east. You enter at the west, past a precinct of retail and service apartments. Blocks 1 to 5 stand along the central spine, and Blocks 6 to 12 sit on a loop road along the south and east, where the land meets a lake edge. Parks, the amenity block, civic amenity land and a future development parcel lie between the towers. The master plan shows it from above.

How many towers and floors does Sobha Chikkagubbi have?

There are 13 towers, rising up to 42 floors, with 4,463 homes between them. On the master plan the homes are grouped into Blocks 1 to 12, and the detailed plan at launch will show how the towers sit within those blocks, the height of each tower and the home types it holds. Since the same home type can sit low or high, ask for the tower and the floor as well as the type when you compare homes.

Is the information on this website final?

No. The project is still in pre-launch, and everything here reflects what was known on 17 October 2026. Areas, layouts, amenities, prices and timelines are indicative and can change, and the floor plans are indicative layouts rather than dimensioned drawings. Your purchase is governed by the cost sheet and the agreement for sale signed with Sobha Limited, so check every figure in those before you pay anything.

Sobha Chikkagubbi homes, sizes and plans

What configurations are available?

Nine home types: 1 BHK Serviced Apartment (100 homes), 1 BHK (301), 2 BHK Luxe (318), 2 BHK Grande (310), 2 BHK + Home Office (310), 3 BHK Luxe (1,428), 3 BHK Grande (1,298), 4 BHK Luxe with three bathrooms (166) and 4 BHK Grande with four bathrooms (166).

Is there a home with space to work from home?

Yes. The 2 BHK + Home Office adds a dedicated home office to a two-bedroom home, the closest match for anyone looking for a 2.5 BHK. There are 310 of them.

What is the carpet area of each Sobha Chikkagubbi home type?

Carpet areas have not been released for any of the nine types; they will come at launch with the dimensioned plans. The only area on record is about 1,940 sq ft, the size quoted with the indicative price, and it is not yet tied to a home type or to a measure. As a rule, carpet area counts only the floor you can use within the walls, and super built-up area is larger because it adds walls, balconies and a portion of the shared spaces.

How do the 2 BHK Luxe, 2 BHK Grande and 2 BHK + Home Office differ?

There are 318 Luxe, 310 Grande and 310 Home Office homes. Going by the indicative layouts, the Luxe and the Grande are both two-bedroom homes with a kitchen, a balcony and a living and dining room, so how they differ in size will be clear once areas are released. The Home Office version includes an extra room set aside for work, which keeps the desk out of the bedrooms.

Should I choose the 3 BHK Luxe or the 3 BHK Grande?

Each is a three-bedroom home with a kitchen and a combined living and dining room. The visible difference in the indicative layouts is outdoor space, with a wide balcony on the Grande; their areas are due at launch. With 1,428 Luxe and 1,298 Grande homes, both give a wide choice of tower and floor, so decide on carpet area, price and outlook once the price sheet is out, not on the format name.

How do the two 4 BHK homes at Sobha Chikkagubbi differ?

Each has four bedrooms, a kitchen and a combined living and dining room. The Luxe comes with three bathrooms and a balcony, the Grande with four bathrooms and more than one balcony. There are 166 of each, so with the 100 serviced 1 BHK apartments they are the smallest groups in the township. If a fourth bathroom matters to your family, ask for the Grande price sheet early.

What is a 1 BHK Serviced Apartment at Sobha Chikkagubbi?

It is a one-bedroom home, with a kitchen, a balcony and a shared living and dining space, offered as a serviced format. In serviced homes, cleaning and upkeep are typically arranged for the resident rather than by them, which suits people who travel often or want less to manage. There are 100 of them, alongside 301 regular 1 BHK homes. Details of the service, including the monthly charge, are due at launch.

Which towers and floors will each home type be in?

That has not been published. The allocation of home types across the 13 towers, floor by floor, is due at launch. On a long site that runs from the entrance precinct in the west to the lake edge in the east, the same type can have quite different surroundings, so ask for the tower plan along with the price sheet and compare the outlook of each option.

Can I get the Sobha Chikkagubbi floor plans before launch?

The indicative 3D layouts of all nine types are on the floor plans page now. They show how the rooms connect but not their sizes. Dimensioned plans with carpet areas will be shared at launch; use the Get Floor Plans button to be sent them as soon as they are out, or the latest project details until then.

Sobha Chikkagubbi price, payment and home loans

What is the price of Sobha Chikkagubbi?

The indicative price is about ₹1.86 Cr for a home of about 1,940 sq ft, with stamp duty and registration extra. Prices for each home type will be announced at launch; register your interest to receive the price list first.

What does the indicative price of about ₹1.86 Cr tell me?

Treat it as one sample figure: about ₹1.86 Cr against an area of about 1,940 sq ft. It is not a starting price, it leaves out stamp duty and registration, and it does not yet say which of the nine home types it belongs to or whether the area is carpet or super built-up. The price of each type, with every charge listed, comes in the cost sheet at launch.

Why is every Sobha Chikkagubbi home type priced on request?

Because the project has not launched yet. Prices for the nine types, from the 1 BHK Serviced Apartment to the 4 BHK Grande, are announced at launch, together with the payment plan. Until then the price table on this website shows each type as on request. Register your interest for the type you want, and the price sheet will be sent to you as soon as it is released.

What payment plan will Sobha Chikkagubbi offer?

The payment plan will be announced with the price list at launch. Under-construction homes are commonly sold on a construction-linked plan, which asks for a booking amount, a second payment when the agreement is signed, and stage-wise instalments as the tower rises. With towers of up to 42 floors, ask how many stages there are and whether your instalments follow the progress of your own tower.

Can I take a home loan for a Sobha Chikkagubbi home?

Yes, subject to your lender’s assessment of your income, your existing loans and the home itself. For a home under construction, the bank usually pays the loan out in parts as each instalment falls due, rather than in one sum. Once the cost sheet is out, share it with two or three lenders and compare their interest rates, fees and the share of the price each will fund.

What is an expression of interest, and is it a booking?

An expression of interest tells the sales team which home type you want before launch, so you are on the list when homes are allotted. Nothing is reserved by it: you choose an actual home, and its price and payment terms are set, only when the project launches. Registering interest on this website costs nothing. If someone asks for money at this stage, find out whether it is refundable and get the terms in writing.

What should I check before paying a token for Sobha Chikkagubbi?

Have the cost sheet of the exact home in writing, with its type, tower, floor and carpet area. Read the payment plan and the agreement for sale, and confirm whether the token is refundable and on what terms. Make the payment to Sobha Limited’s own account, never in cash or to an individual, and keep proof of it. This website never collects payments.

Are parking, clubhouse and maintenance charges extra?

The cost sheet at launch will show this line by line. The township plans 5,029 parking spaces; check whether one comes with your home or is priced separately, and whether extra spaces can be bought. Look also for any clubhouse or amenity charge, advance maintenance and the period it covers, and the taxes that apply, since each of these can add to the base price.

Sobha Chikkagubbi location and connectivity

Where is Sobha Chikkagubbi located?

Across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages in Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East, near Hennur Road. Bhartiya City is about 3.4 km away, Kristu Jayanti University about 4.9 km, Thanisandra Railway Station about 6.5 km and Manyata Tech Park about 9 km.

What is the full address of Sobha Chikkagubbi?

Kannur and Chikkagubbi Villages, Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East Taluk, Bengaluru Urban District, Karnataka. In everyday terms, it is near Hennur Road in North-East Bengaluru. The main access is planned at the western end of the site, next to the retail and service-apartment precinct, and the map pin marks the township for planning your drive.

Is Sobha Chikkagubbi on Hennur Main Road?

No. Its land is in Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages near Hennur Road, not on Hennur Main Road itself. Compared with Purva Skye, which is on the main road, Sobha Chikkagubbi is nearer Bhartiya City (about 3.4 km against about 6.2 km) and Yelahanka Junction Railway Station (about 9.6 km against about 15.4 km), and farther from Manyata Tech Park (about 9 km against about 5.7 km).

Which offices are close to Sobha Chikkagubbi?

Bhartiya City is the closest of the places we list, about 3.4 km away, with Manyata Tech Park at about 9 km. Closer still, the commercial precinct at the western entrance of the township is planned to include offices, though whether that space will be leased or sold has not been announced. Distances are approximate driving distances, so time your commute at the hour you would travel.

Which schools and colleges are near Sobha Chikkagubbi?

For school-age children, VIBGYOR High School, Hennur is about 6.1 km away; for college, Kristu Jayanti University is about 4.9 km. The township plan also reserves space for education and learning; which institution will use it, and when, is not yet known. Parents comparing homes here should do the school run once during a site visit, at the time it would normally happen.

How far is the nearest hospital from Sobha Chikkagubbi?

The closest hospital on our list is Cratis Hospital in Kothanur, about 5.4 km away; Manipal Hospital, Hebbal is about 12.4 km. The township plan includes space for medical and health services, with details to be confirmed as it develops. For a household with elderly parents, drive to Kothanur once during your visit to see how long it takes.

How is Sobha Chikkagubbi connected by rail and road?

Two railway stations are within reach: Thanisandra, about 6.5 km away, and Yelahanka Junction, about 9.6 km. For road journeys, the drive to Hebbal Flyover is about 15.3 km. All three are approximate driving distances, and the time each trip takes changes through the day, so check the live estimate on the map for the hours you would travel before you decide.

Is Sobha Chikkagubbi convenient for frequent flyers?

The drive to Kempegowda International Airport, Terminal 1 is about 20.9 km, a little shorter than from Purva Skye on Hennur Main Road, at about 23.9 km. If you fly often, try the drive at the hour of your usual flights, since traffic varies by the hour, and compare it with the drive from your present home.

Sobha Chikkagubbi amenities and daily life

What amenities are planned?

A clubhouse and an amenity block, with space set aside for education, banking, post and communication, medical care, recreation and business services, parks and landscaped open spaces across about 37% green cover, and a precinct of offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants.

What will the Sobha Chikkagubbi clubhouse have?

The clubhouse is planned with a double-height lounge that opens onto the gardens, and a separate amenity block adds more shared space. The project illustrations include a learning space, an indoor games room, a health room and a business centre. A complete facility list is due at launch; ask then for the clubhouse floor plan and the date each facility is expected to open.

Is the commercial precinct only for residents?

That has not been announced. The precinct, with its offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants, is placed at the western entrance by the main access, the part of the site that visitors and office-goers reach first. Whether it will be open to the public, and how its traffic will be kept apart from the homes, are worth asking at launch, especially if you are choosing a home in the first blocks.

Is there space for children to play at Sobha Chikkagubbi?

Yes. Parks and landscaped open spaces are planned between the towers, and the project imagery shows a sports court and a play lawn under shade trees. About 37% of the site is set aside as green cover, backed by a tree-planting programme. When the landscape plan is released, check the walk from your preferred block to the nearest play area.

Will homes at Sobha Chikkagubbi look onto the lake?

On the master plan, a lake edge runs along the eastern side of the site, and the loop road serving Blocks 6 to 12 follows the south and east. Which towers and floors will face the lake has not been published. If a lake outlook matters to you, ask for the tower plans at launch and check which way the balcony of your chosen home faces, and what lies between it and the water.

What is dual plumbing, and how does Sobha Chikkagubbi save water?

Dual plumbing means two water networks: one for fresh water and one for treated water, which goes to uses such as flushing. At Sobha Chikkagubbi it is paired with on-site sewage treatment by the activated sludge process, reuse of the treated water and rainwater harvesting. Together these reduce the fresh water the township needs, though running the treatment plant will be part of the maintenance cost.

What will maintenance cost at Sobha Chikkagubbi?

The maintenance charge has not been announced; it will be set out at launch. In a township of 4,463 homes with a clubhouse, an amenity block, parks and a sewage treatment plant, ask what the charge covers, whether an advance is collected and for how many months, and who will manage the township after handover, the developer or a residents’ association.
