Sobha Chikkagubbi clubhouse and amenity block

The clubhouse and the amenity block are the two shared buildings for residents. The pictures show a double-height clubhouse lounge that opens onto the gardens, and the gallery has illustrations of the learning space, the indoor games room, the health room and the business centre.

The full list of facilities, their sizes and when each opens will be shared at launch. If particular facilities matter to you, ask for the clubhouse plan before you choose a home. With 4,463 homes sharing these buildings, it is also worth asking how bookings and timings for the shared spaces will work.

Sobha Chikkagubbi services inside the township

Beyond leisure, the plan sets aside space for everyday services: education and learning, medical and health services, banking, and post and communication, alongside business and meeting spaces. In a township of 4,463 homes, these help routine errands stay inside the community instead of needing a drive out.

Civic amenity land is also marked on the master plan. Which services open first, and who will run them, will be confirmed as the township develops.

Sobha Chikkagubbi retail and dining precinct

Offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants are grouped in a precinct of their own at the western entrance, by the main access. Keeping them together means residents have shops and restaurants close by, while the residential blocks further along the site are set back from that activity.

The position is worth weighing when you choose a home: homes nearer the west are closer to the shops, and homes further east are closer to the lake edge. Families who want quiet streets may lean towards the blocks set back from the entrance, while those who want shops at the door may prefer the west.

Parks, gardens and green cover at Sobha Chikkagubbi

About 37% of the land is planned as green cover, with parks and landscaped open spaces between the towers and a tree-planting programme. The pictures show a garden walk through lawns and trees beside the towers, and a sports court and play lawn under shade trees.

Along the east, the site meets a lake edge. For families, the play lawn and the sports court give children room to play inside the township. At launch, ask how the open spaces are spread between the towers and which homes look onto them.

Sobha Chikkagubbi water systems, explained

On-site sewage treatment at Sobha Chikkagubbi uses the activated sludge process, in which air and microbes break down waste water so that it can be treated and used again. Dual plumbing usually means a second set of pipes that carries treated water for uses such as flushing, kept separate from fresh water.

With the reuse of treated water on site and rainwater harvesting, these reduce how much fresh water the township draws. They also show up in maintenance, so ask how the treatment plant will be run, and paid for, after handover.

Parking at Sobha Chikkagubbi

The township plans 5,029 parking spaces. How spaces are allotted, whether one comes with each home, and the charge for any additional space will be set out in the cost sheet at launch. With 13 towers on a long site, also ask where your space will be in relation to your tower, and whether visitor parking is set aside.

Sobha Chikkagubbi amenities FAQs

Does Sobha Chikkagubbi have a clubhouse?

Yes. A clubhouse and a separate amenity block are planned; the full list of facilities will be shared at launch.

Will there be shops and restaurants inside Sobha Chikkagubbi?

Yes. Shops and restaurants are planned in a commercial precinct, with offices and service apartments, at the western end of the site.

How much green space is planned at Sobha Chikkagubbi?

About 37% of the land is planned as green cover, with parks, landscaped gardens and a tree-planting programme.

How many parking spaces are planned?

5,029 parking spaces across the township.

Does Sobha Chikkagubbi plan to save water?

Yes: on-site sewage treatment, dual plumbing, reuse of treated water and rainwater harvesting.
