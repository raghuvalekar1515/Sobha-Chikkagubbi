How Sobha Limited builds

Sobha is known for building end to end. Rather than relying only on outside contractors, it runs its own contracting and manufacturing, which covers interiors, glazing, metal works and concrete products.

For a buyer, this means more of the work on a home, from the concrete to the fittings, is done within the developer’s own operations. At launch, ask how the finishes of the Sobha Chikkagubbi homes will be specified in the agreement, so you know exactly what is delivered.

Sobha Limited’s business segments

Sobha works across residential, contractual, non-residential and commercial segments. Its homes take several forms: apartments, villas, row houses and plotted developments, alongside commercial spaces and townships.

Sobha Chikkagubbi brings several of these together. It is a township, its homes are apartments, and it includes a commercial precinct of offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants, so the homes and the commercial spaces are planned together as one development.

Where Sobha Limited builds

Sobha builds in 14 cities across India: Bengaluru, Chennai, Coimbatore, GIFT City Gandhinagar, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Kochi, Kozhikode, Mumbai, Mysuru, Pune, Thiruvananthapuram and Thrissur.

Outside Bengaluru, its projects include Sobha Altus in Sector 106, Gurugram, Sobha Nesara in Pune and Sobha Lake Edge in Thrissur.

Sobha Limited in Bengaluru

Sobha’s Bengaluru homes include Sobha Neopolis in Panathur, Sobha Windsor in Whitefield, Sobha Oakshire in Devanahalli, Sobha Townpark and Sobha OneWorld.

Among the Sobha projects we market in the city are Sobha Mullur at Mullur, near Kodathi, on Sarjapur Road, and Sobha Biopolis at Mullur, off Sarjapur Road. Sobha Chikkagubbi adds a township near Hennur Road in North-East Bengaluru.

Sobha Chikkagubbi in Sobha’s portfolio

Sobha Chikkagubbi is a township of about 50.45 acres across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages, with 4,463 homes in 13 towers of up to 42 floors, a commercial precinct, a clubhouse, an amenity block and about 37% green cover.

Its range of homes is wide: serviced and regular 1 BHK homes, 2 BHK homes with a home office option, 3 BHK homes that make up more than half of the community, and 4 BHK homes with three or four bathrooms. See the overview for the full project facts.

Buying a Sobha home through a channel partner

This website is operated by a channel partner. It is not the developer’s website, and it does not accept bookings or payments. Its role is to share information about the project, send you the brochure or the latest project details, arrange site visits and put you in touch with the sales process.

Your purchase itself is with Sobha Limited. The price, the home allotted to you and your rights are set by the cost sheet and the agreement for sale you sign with the developer, so read both before you pay anything. If anyone asks you to pay a token into a personal account, or to a channel partner, decline and check with the developer first.

What to ask Sobha Limited at launch

When Sobha Chikkagubbi launches, these are the questions worth putting to the developer before you choose a home:

  • Which tower your home is in, and the order in which the towers will be built.
  • How the finishes and fittings will be specified in the agreement for sale.
  • The payment plan, and whether it follows the construction of your tower.
  • Who will maintain the township after handover, and what it will cost.
  • What is planned for the commercial precinct and the future development parcel, and when.
