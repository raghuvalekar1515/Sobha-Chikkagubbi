Sobha Chikkagubbi Project Overview

Sobha Chikkagubbi is an upcoming mixed-use township by Sobha across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages, near Hennur Road in North-East Bengaluru. Spread over about 50.45 acres, it plans 4,463 homes in 13 towers rising up to 42 floors: serviced and regular 1 BHK apartments, 2 BHK homes in Luxe, Grande and Home Office formats, and 3 BHK and 4 BHK residences in Luxe and Grande formats. Offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants sit in a precinct of their own, while about 37% of the land is planned as green cover, with parks, a clubhouse and an amenity block beside a lake edge.

Sobha Chikkagubbi Master Plan

The master plan arranges the township along a long site. A retail and service-apartment precinct with the main access opens it at the west, residential Blocks 1 to 5 follow the main spine, and Blocks 6 to 12 turn around a southern and eastern loop road. Parks and open spaces, an amenity block, civic amenities and a future development parcel sit between the towers, with a lake edge along the east.

Sobha Chikkagubbi Homes and Sizes in Brief

Sobha Chikkagubbi is built around family homes. Of the 4,463 apartments planned, 1,428 are 3 BHK Luxe and 1,298 are 3 BHK Grande, so three-bedroom buyers get the widest pick of towers and floors.

Smaller households can start with the 1 BHK (301 homes) or the 1 BHK Serviced Apartment (100 homes), or move up to a 2 BHK in Luxe (318), Grande (310) or Home Office (310) form, the last with a separate room for work. At the top of the range, the 4 BHK Luxe and 4 BHK Grande differ mainly in bathrooms, three against four, with 166 homes each.

Carpet areas are not out yet; the one size on record is about 1,940 sq ft, quoted with the indicative price. The indicative layout of every type is on the Sobha Chikkagubbi floor plans page.

Sobha Chikkagubbi Location in Brief

Sobha Chikkagubbi lies in Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages, near Hennur Road in the north-east of the city. For work, Bhartiya City is the nearest listed destination at about 3.4 km, Manyata Tech Park is about 9 km off, and the township adds offices of its own.

For families, the listed schools and colleges are VIBGYOR High School, Hennur (about 6.1 km) and Kristu Jayanti University (about 4.9 km), and the hospitals are Cratis Hospital, Kothanur (about 5.4 km) and Manipal Hospital, Hebbal (about 12.4 km). The two stations within reach are Thanisandra (about 6.5 km) and Yelahanka Junction (about 9.6 km), and the airport, Kempegowda International Airport, Terminal 1, is about 20.9 km by road.

These are approximate driving distances; try your own routes at the hours you travel. The Sobha Chikkagubbi location page has the full table, the address and a map.

The single figure published so far is an indicative price of about ₹1.86 Cr for about 1,940 sq ft; stamp duty and registration come on top. All nine home types stay on request until launch, when the price list and the payment plan are released together.

Only then do you pay a booking amount to Sobha Limited and sign the agreement for sale; construction-linked instalments, which a home loan can fund, usually follow.

Sobha Chikkagubbi Amenities in Brief

Sobha Chikkagubbi plans much of daily life inside the township. Residents share a clubhouse, with a double-height lounge looking onto the gardens, and a separate amenity block. Land is also kept for learning and education, healthcare, banking, post and communication, and business and meeting spaces, while offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants cluster at the western entrance.

Outdoors, green cover is planned on about 37% of the land, taking in parks, landscaped gardens, a sports court and play lawn, with a lake edge to the east. Sewage is treated on site and the water reused, with dual plumbing and rainwater harvesting, and 5,029 parking spaces are planned. Which facilities open with the first towers is still to be confirmed. The Sobha Chikkagubbi amenities page has the complete list.

As of June 2026, Sobha counted 589+ developments delivered, adding up to 152.69 million sq ft of built space, and it builds in 14 cities, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and Pune among them. Sobha Chikkagubbi How It Compares

Weighing a smaller project on the Hennur Road corridor too? Compare Sobha Chikkagubbi with Purva Skye, a Puravankara project on Hennur Main Road. Scale differs first: 4,463 homes on about 50.45 acres here, against 192 homes in two towers on about 3.03 acres there.

Purva Skye has 3, 4 and 5 BHK homes only, so of the two, only Sobha Chikkagubbi offers a 1 BHK, a serviced 1 BHK or a two-bedroom home with a separate home office. Purva Skye is nearer Manyata Tech Park, about 5.7 km against about 9 km; Sobha Chikkagubbi is nearer Bhartiya City and the airport.
