Sobha Chikkagubbi Project Overview

Sobha Chikkagubbi is an upcoming mixed-use township by Sobha across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages, near Hennur Road in North-East Bengaluru. Spread over about 50.45 acres, it plans 4,463 homes in 13 towers rising up to 42 floors: serviced and regular 1 BHK apartments, 2 BHK homes in Luxe, Grande and Home Office formats, and 3 BHK and 4 BHK residences in Luxe and Grande formats. Offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants sit in a precinct of their own, while about 37% of the land is planned as green cover, with parks, a clubhouse and an amenity block beside a lake edge.

Sobha Chikkagubbi Master Plan

The master plan arranges the township along a long site. A retail and service-apartment precinct with the main access opens it at the west, residential Blocks 1 to 5 follow the main spine, and Blocks 6 to 12 turn around a southern and eastern loop road. Parks and open spaces, an amenity block, civic amenities and a future development parcel sit between the towers, with a lake edge along the east.

Where is Sobha Chikkagubbi located?

Across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages in Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East, near Hennur Road. Bhartiya City is about 3.4 km away, Kristu Jayanti University about 4.9 km, Thanisandra Railway Station about 6.5 km and Manyata Tech Park about 9 km.

Is there a home with space to work from home?

Yes. The 2 BHK + Home Office adds a dedicated home office to a two-bedroom home, the closest match for anyone looking for a 2.5 BHK. There are 310 of them.

What amenities are planned?

A clubhouse and an amenity block, with space set aside for education, banking, post and communication, medical care, recreation and business services, parks and landscaped open spaces across about 37% green cover, and a precinct of offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants.

Who is the developer?

Sobha Limited, founded in 1995 by PNC Menon and known for building end to end with its own contracting and manufacturing, from interiors to glazing, metal works and concrete products. Sobha builds in 14 cities across India; its Bengaluru homes include SOBHA Neopolis, SOBHA Townpark and SOBHA OneWorld.
