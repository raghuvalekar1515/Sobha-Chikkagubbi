Sobha Chikkagubbi 1 BHK and 1 BHK Serviced Apartment

The 1 BHK formats are the compact end of the plan. The 1 BHK, with 301 homes, has a bedroom, a living and dining room, a kitchen and a balcony. The 1 BHK Serviced Apartment, with 100 homes, has a bedroom, a living and dining area, a kitchen and a balcony, and is the smallest group of homes in the township.

A serviced apartment is usually run with services such as housekeeping and upkeep organised for residents. How the Sobha Chikkagubbi serviced format will be run, and what it will cost, will be confirmed at launch; ask for both, along with the carpet area of each 1 BHK format.

Sobha Chikkagubbi 2 BHK: Luxe, Grande and Home Office

The 2 BHK comes in Luxe, Grande and Home Office formats, with 318, 310 and 310 homes. In the indicative layouts, the Luxe and the Grande both show two bedrooms, a living and dining room, a kitchen and a balcony, so the difference between them will show in the areas and room sizes at launch.

The Home Office format adds a separate room for work to the two bedrooms. It suits a household where one or both partners work from home and want to keep the second bedroom for children, parents or guests, and it is the nearest match to a 2.5 BHK in the plan.

Sobha Chikkagubbi 3 BHK: Luxe and Grande

The 3 BHK is the heart of Sobha Chikkagubbi. The 3 BHK Luxe has 1,428 homes and the 3 BHK Grande 1,298, together more than half of the community.

Both layouts have three bedrooms, a living and dining room and a kitchen. The Grande layout shows a wide balcony where the Luxe shows a balcony; the area of each will be confirmed at launch. With this many homes in the plan, 3 BHK buyers will have the most homes to choose from, so decide early which part of the site and which floors you prefer.

Sobha Chikkagubbi 4 BHK: Luxe and Grande

There are two 4 BHK residences, with 166 homes each. Both have four bedrooms, a living and dining room and a kitchen. The 4 BHK Luxe has three bathrooms and a balcony; the 4 BHK Grande has four bathrooms and balconies.

The bathroom count is the clearest difference. If every bedroom needs its own bathroom, look at the Grande; if one shared bathroom works for your family, the Luxe is the other choice. With 166 homes in each, the 4 BHK formats are much scarcer than the 3 BHK, so ask for the 4 BHK price sheet early.

How to read the Sobha Chikkagubbi floor plans

The layouts on this page are indicative 3D views. They are good for understanding how the rooms connect: where you enter, whether the kitchen opens to the dining area, which rooms open onto the balcony, and how the bedrooms sit in relation to the living room.

They do not give dimensions or areas. At launch, ask for the dimensioned 2D plan of the exact home, with its carpet area, the size of each room and the direction the balcony faces. In a township of 13 towers of up to 42 floors, the same type can look onto a garden, a road or the lake edge, so check the floor plan against the master plan too.

Sobha Chikkagubbi floor plan FAQs

What sizes are the Sobha Chikkagubbi homes?

The area of each home type will be shared at launch. The one figure so far is an indicative price of about ₹1.86 Cr for about 1,940 sq ft.

What is the difference between Luxe and Grande?

In the indicative layouts, the 3 BHK Grande shows a wide balcony and the 4 BHK Grande a fourth bathroom and balconies. The areas of each format will show the rest at launch.

Is there a 2.5 BHK at Sobha Chikkagubbi?

Which Sobha Chikkagubbi home type has the most homes?

The 3 BHK Luxe, with 1,428 homes, followed by the 3 BHK Grande with 1,298.

Are the Sobha Chikkagubbi floor plans final?

No. They are indicative layouts, and detailed floor plans with areas will be shared at launch.
