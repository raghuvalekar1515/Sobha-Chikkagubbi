Sobha Chikkagubbi address explained

The full address is Kannur and Chikkagubbi Villages, Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East Taluk, Bengaluru Urban District, Karnataka. A hobli is a group of villages within a taluk, so the address places the township in the eastern taluk of Bengaluru Urban District, on the Hennur Road side of North-East Bengaluru.

Because the site is long and runs from west to east, where you live inside it matters too. The main access is at the western end, beside the retail and service-apartment precinct, and the lake edge is along the east. Open the map to see the site and plan your routes.

Sobha Chikkagubbi for work: Bhartiya City and Manyata Tech Park

For work, Bhartiya City is about 3.4 km away, the nearest landmark in the table above, and Manyata Tech Park is about 9 km.

The township also plans its own commercial precinct with offices, so for some residents work could be within the township itself. Whether office space there will be for lease or for sale will be confirmed later.

Schools, colleges and hospitals near Sobha Chikkagubbi

For education, Kristu Jayanti University is about 4.9 km away and VIBGYOR High School, Hennur about 6.1 km. For healthcare, Cratis Hospital in Kothanur is about 5.4 km away and Manipal Hospital, Hebbal about 12.4 km.

Inside the township, the plan sets aside space for education and learning and for medical and health services. What will run there, and from when, will be confirmed as the project develops, so families should plan around the schools and hospitals nearby for now.

Sobha Chikkagubbi rail and road connections

Two railway stations are within reach: Thanisandra Railway Station about 6.5 km away and Yelahanka Junction Railway Station about 9.6 km. By road, Hebbal Flyover is about 15.3 km away.

These are approximate driving distances. Travel time depends on the hour of the day, so check live travel times on the map and try the drive you will make most often before you decide.

Sobha Chikkagubbi to Kempegowda International Airport

Kempegowda International Airport, Terminal 1 is about 20.9 km away, the farthest point in the table. For frequent flyers, that keeps the airport within a drive of home without living right beside it. As with the other distances, check the live travel time at the hour you usually fly.
