Sobha Chikkagubbi Master Plan

The master plan arranges the township along a long site. A retail and service-apartment precinct with the main access opens it at the west, residential Blocks 1 to 5 follow the main spine, and Blocks 6 to 12 turn around a southern and eastern loop road. Parks and open spaces, an amenity block, civic amenities and a future development parcel sit between the towers, with a lake edge along the east.

How to read the Sobha Chikkagubbi master plan

A master plan is a map of the whole site from above. Start with three things: where you enter, how the internal roads run, and where the open spaces are. Then find the block you are interested in and see what lies around it: a road, a park, the amenity block or the edge of the site. Match each label to the legend before drawing conclusions.

The Sobha Chikkagubbi plan is an aerial view of a long site that reads from west to east. It shows the retail and service-apartment precinct, the residential blocks, the parks, the amenity block and the lake edge. A plan at pre-launch can still change, so check the plan attached to your agreement when you buy.

Sobha Chikkagubbi master plan: the western entrance

The township opens at the west, where the main access meets a precinct of retail and service apartments. This is the public face of the site: the part that visitors, shoppers and people coming to the offices will see first. Placing it here means the busy, commercial part of the township is met at its edge, before the residential blocks begin.

For residents, the entrance precinct puts shops and service apartments at the start of the journey home. Homes in the first blocks are closest to it; homes further east trade that convenience for more distance from the entrance.

Sobha Chikkagubbi Blocks 1 to 5 on the main spine

From the entrance, residential Blocks 1 to 5 line the main spine, the central route into the township. These are the first homes you reach after the precinct.

Living on the spine means a direct route to the entrance and the shops. When the detailed plan is out, look at how far each block sits from the road and which way its homes face, and ask which towers are in each block and the order in which they will be built.

Sobha Chikkagubbi Blocks 6 to 12 and the loop road

Beyond the spine, Blocks 6 to 12 turn around a loop road that runs along the south and east of the site. A loop road gives these blocks an approach of their own, away from the entrance.

The blocks on the eastern side are the ones nearest the lake edge. The master plan labels residential Blocks 1 to 12, while the township plans 13 towers; how the towers sit within the blocks will be clear in the detailed plan at launch.

Open spaces and shared buildings between the towers

Parks and open spaces, the amenity block and civic amenity land are placed between the towers rather than pushed to one edge. About 37% of the land is planned as green cover, spread through the township rather than kept in a single park.

On the plan, find the green areas nearest the block on your shortlist and the path from it to the amenity block. For families with children, open space a short walk from home matters more than the total figure. At launch, ask for the landscape plan, which shows these spaces in more detail.

The lake edge and the future development parcel

Along the east, the township meets a lake edge. Homes facing east may look towards it; ask which towers and floors have that outlook when the tower plans are out.

The master plan also marks a future development parcel. What will be built there, and when, has not been announced; ask about it at launch, as it may affect the outlook of the homes nearby.

Sobha Chikkagubbi master plan FAQs

Where is the main entrance of Sobha Chikkagubbi?

At the western end of the site, beside the retail and service-apartment precinct.

How many residential blocks does the Sobha Chikkagubbi master plan show?

The plan labels Blocks 1 to 12: Blocks 1 to 5 along the main spine and Blocks 6 to 12 around the loop road. The township plans 13 towers.

Is there a lake near Sobha Chikkagubbi?

Yes. The master plan shows a lake edge along the east of the site.

What is the future development parcel?

A parcel marked on the master plan for later development. Its use has not been announced and will be confirmed later.
