Sobha Chikkagubbi price and the area behind it

Apartment prices are quoted against an area, and that area can be measured in more than one way. Carpet area is the floor you can actually use inside the home’s walls: the bedrooms, living and dining room, kitchen and bathrooms. Super built-up area adds the walls, the balconies and a share of the common areas, such as lobbies, corridors, lift lobbies and the clubhouse.

At Sobha Chikkagubbi this matters because the only figure so far, about 1,940 sq ft, does not yet say which measure it uses. A township with 13 towers of up to 42 floors, a clubhouse and an amenity block has a lot of common area to share out. When you set the ₹1.86 Cr indicative price against another project, compare the two on the same measure.

Sobha Chikkagubbi pre-launch: what an expression of interest means

Before a project launches, a developer may take an expression of interest: you name the home type you want and sometimes pay an amount to hold a place in the queue for allotment at launch. It is not a booking. The price, the exact home and the payment plan are fixed only at launch, when you accept a cost sheet. If an amount is asked for, ask whether it is refundable.

At Sobha Chikkagubbi, an early place matters more in the smaller groups of homes: 100 in the 1 BHK Serviced Apartment and 166 in each 4 BHK format. The 3 BHK Luxe and Grande, with 1,428 and 1,298 homes, leave more to choose from. This website records interest only; it does not take bookings or payments.

Construction-linked payment plans, explained for Sobha Chikkagubbi

The payment plan for Sobha Chikkagubbi will be shared with the price list at launch. Many homes bought under construction are paid for on a construction-linked plan: an amount at booking, another at the agreement, and the rest in instalments as the building reaches stages such as the foundation, each floor slab and the finishing work.

With towers of up to 42 floors, the slab stages of a tower run over a long period, and the instalments are spread out with them. Ask for the schedule of the tower your home is in, and confirm whether each instalment follows that tower’s progress or a fixed calendar.

Home loans for a Sobha Chikkagubbi home

Most buyers pay for part of a home with a loan. The bank decides how much it will lend from your income, your existing loans and the value of the home, and the rest is your own contribution.

For a home bought under construction, such as one at Sobha Chikkagubbi, the bank releases the loan in parts as the construction-linked instalments fall due, and until handover you usually pay interest only on the amount released so far. Take the cost sheet to your bank: it shows the agreement value the loan is based on, and the charges you will pay from your own funds.
