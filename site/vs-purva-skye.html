Sobha Chikkagubbi vs Purva Skye

Sobha Chikkagubbi and Purva Skye are both pre-launch apartment projects on the Hennur Road corridor of North Bengaluru, but they are very different in scale. This guide puts them side by side so you can see which one fits what you are looking for.

Sobha Chikkagubbi vs Purva Skye: the short answer

Sobha Chikkagubbi is a mixed-use township by Sobha Limited: 4,463 homes in 13 towers of up to 42 floors on about 50.45 acres, with nine home types from a serviced 1 BHK to a 4 BHK, a commercial precinct and about 37% green cover.

Purva Skye is a two-tower project by Puravankara on Hennur Main Road: 192 homes of 3, 4 and 5 BHK on about 3.03 acres, each tower with three basements, a ground floor and 24 upper floors. One is a neighbourhood in itself; the other is a compact address on the main road.

Scale: a township against two towers

The clearest difference is scale. Sobha Chikkagubbi plans 4,463 homes on about 50.45 acres; Purva Skye plans 192 homes on about 3.03 acres. A township brings its own clubhouse, amenity block, commercial precinct and civic amenity land, built across 13 towers. A two-tower project has far fewer households sharing the site and its facilities.

Both are high-rise. The Sobha Chikkagubbi towers rise up to 42 floors, and each Purva Skye tower has three basements, a ground floor and 24 upper floors.

Home types: Sobha Chikkagubbi vs Purva Skye

Sobha Chikkagubbi has the wider range of the two, from a 1 BHK Serviced Apartment and a 1 BHK through 2 BHK Luxe, Grande and Home Office formats to 3 BHK and 4 BHK homes in Luxe and Grande formats. The 3 BHK homes make up more than half of the township.

Purva Skye starts at 3 BHK and goes up to 5 BHK, with 4 BHK homes in between. It has no 1 BHK or 2 BHK, and Sobha Chikkagubbi has no 5 BHK. Sizes for both will be shared at launch.

Amenities and everyday life

Sobha Chikkagubbi plans a clubhouse, an amenity block, space for education, medical care, banking and post, a precinct of offices, shops and restaurants, about 37% green cover and 5,029 parking spaces.

Purva Skye’s amenities will be shared with its master plan at launch. Around it, Cratis Hospital is about 0.4 km away and VIBGYOR High School about 1.1 km, so it leans on the neighbourhood of Hennur, which is well served.

Who each one suits

  • Sobha Chikkagubbi suits buyers who want a home inside a large planned township with shops, offices and services on site; buyers looking for a 1 BHK, a serviced 1 BHK, a 2 BHK or a 2 BHK with a home office, which Purva Skye does not offer; families choosing among many 3 BHK homes; and anyone who works near Bhartiya City or flies often.
  • Purva Skye suits buyers who want a 3, 4 or 5 BHK in a two-tower project of 192 homes on Hennur Main Road itself, close to Manyata Tech Park, Kristu Jayanti University, VIBGYOR High School and Cratis Hospital, and households that need a 5 BHK.

Both are in pre-launch, so neither has shared the price or size of each home yet. Ask for both price sheets at launch and compare them on the same area measure. Purva Skye has its own website at purvaskye.info.

Sobha Chikkagubbi vs Purva Skye summary

Choose Sobha Chikkagubbi for a township of about 50.45 acres with nine home types and facilities on site; choose Purva Skye for a two-tower project of 3, 4 and 5 BHK homes on Hennur Main Road, closer to Manyata Tech Park. Both are in pre-launch, with the price and size of each home type due at launch.

Sobha Chikkagubbi vs Purva Skye FAQs

Which is bigger, Sobha Chikkagubbi or Purva Skye?

Sobha Chikkagubbi, with about 50.45 acres and 4,463 homes, against about 3.03 acres and 192 homes at Purva Skye.

Which is closer to Manyata Tech Park?

Purva Skye, at about 5.7 km. Sobha Chikkagubbi is about 9 km away.

Does Purva Skye have 1 BHK or 2 BHK homes?

No. Purva Skye offers 3, 4 and 5 BHK homes. Sobha Chikkagubbi offers 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK homes in nine home types.

Are Sobha Chikkagubbi and Purva Skye both in pre-launch?

Yes. Both are in pre-launch, and the price and size of each home type will be shared at launch.

Who are the developers of Sobha Chikkagubbi and Purva Skye?

Sobha Chikkagubbi is by Sobha Limited and Purva Skye is by Puravankara.
