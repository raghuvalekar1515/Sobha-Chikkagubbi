Sobha Chikkagubbi Project Overview

Sobha Chikkagubbi is an upcoming mixed-use township by Sobha across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages, near Hennur Road in North-East Bengaluru. Spread over about 50.45 acres, it plans 4,463 homes in 13 towers rising up to 42 floors: serviced and regular 1 BHK apartments, 2 BHK homes in Luxe, Grande and Home Office formats, and 3 BHK and 4 BHK residences in Luxe and Grande formats. Offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants sit in a precinct of their own, while about 37% of the land is planned as green cover, with parks, a clubhouse and an amenity block beside a lake edge.
