Sobha Chikkagubbi Project Overview

Sobha Chikkagubbi is an upcoming mixed-use township by Sobha across Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages, near Hennur Road in North-East Bengaluru. Spread over about 50.45 acres, it plans 4,463 homes in 13 towers rising up to 42 floors: serviced and regular 1 BHK apartments, 2 BHK homes in Luxe, Grande and Home Office formats, and 3 BHK and 4 BHK residences in Luxe and Grande formats. Offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants sit in a precinct of their own, while about 37% of the land is planned as green cover, with parks, a clubhouse and an amenity block beside a lake edge.

Sobha Chikkagubbi: a township, not a single block

Sobha Chikkagubbi is planned as a mixed-use township rather than a standalone apartment block. On about 50.45 acres in Kannur and Chikkagubbi villages, the homes share the site with a commercial precinct, a clubhouse, an amenity block, civic amenity land and parks, so a good part of daily life is planned to happen inside the township.

That scale changes what a buyer should look at. With 4,463 homes in 13 towers of up to 42 floors, the choice is not only which home type to buy but also which tower, which floor and which part of a long site, from the retail precinct at the western entrance to the lake edge along the east.

Sobha Chikkagubbi homes in brief

There are nine home types, from a 1 BHK Serviced Apartment to a 4 BHK Grande with four bathrooms. Family homes lead the mix: the 3 BHK Luxe (1,428 homes) and the 3 BHK Grande (1,298 homes) together make up more than half of the community.

Around them sit smaller and larger formats: 100 serviced 1 BHK apartments and 301 regular 1 BHK homes, 2 BHK homes in Luxe, Grande and Home Office formats, and 166 homes in each of the two 4 BHK formats. The floor plans page shows the indicative layout of every type.

Sobha Chikkagubbi township planning

Within the township, the commercial precinct of offices, service apartments, shops and restaurants sits at the western entrance, apart from the residential blocks. Parks, the amenity block and civic amenity land are spread between the towers rather than gathered in one corner, and the eastern side of the site meets a lake edge.

The plan also marks a future development parcel; what it will hold has not been announced and will be confirmed later. The master plan page walks through the layout block by block, and the amenities page covers the shared facilities.

Sobha Chikkagubbi location at a glance

The township is in Bidarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru East Taluk, near Hennur Road in North-East Bengaluru. Bhartiya City is about 3.4 km away, Thanisandra Railway Station about 6.5 km, Manyata Tech Park about 9 km and Kempegowda International Airport, Terminal 1 about 20.9 km. Schools, hospitals and travel routes are on the location page.

Who Sobha Chikkagubbi suits

  • Families who want room to grow. The 3 BHK Luxe and Grande make up the bulk of the township, so they offer the most homes to choose from.
  • People who work from home. The 2 BHK + Home Office (310 homes) gives a separate room for work without stepping up to a 3 BHK.
  • First homes and smaller households. The 1 BHK (301 homes) and the 1 BHK Serviced Apartment (100 homes) are the compact formats in the plan.
  • Larger households. The 4 BHK Luxe with three bathrooms and the 4 BHK Grande with four, 166 homes each.
  • Professionals on this side of the city. Bhartiya City is about 3.4 km away and Manyata Tech Park about 9 km, and the township has offices of its own in the commercial precinct.

Sobha Chikkagubbi overview FAQs

Is Sobha Chikkagubbi a township or an apartment project?

Both. It is a mixed-use township of about 50.45 acres whose homes are apartments: 4,463 of them in 13 towers, alongside a commercial precinct, a clubhouse, an amenity block and civic amenity land.

How many home types does Sobha Chikkagubbi have?

Nine: 1 BHK Serviced Apartment, 1 BHK, 2 BHK Luxe, 2 BHK Grande, 2 BHK + Home Office, 3 BHK Luxe, 3 BHK Grande, 4 BHK Luxe and 4 BHK Grande. The 3 BHK homes make up more than half of the community.

How tall are the Sobha Chikkagubbi towers?

The 13 towers rise up to 42 floors. Which home types sit in which tower, and on which floors, will be confirmed at launch.

How much of Sobha Chikkagubbi is green?

About 37% of the land is planned as green cover, with parks, landscaped open spaces and a tree-planting programme.

What stage is Sobha Chikkagubbi at?

Pre-launch. The details here are as of 17 October 2026; the price of each home type, the areas and the payment plan will be shared at launch.
